Our story

A legacy built
on hospitality.

Founded in Europe in 1970 by Heidi Piccard as a family business, SWISZTA began with a simple yet powerful vision — to provide hotels with service solutions that consistently exceed guest expectations.

Today, our European heritage and local expertise guide everything we do as we support hotels across Australia and New Zealand.
